# Photography guide

For dense hydrological tables, image quality usually contributes more to accuracy than changing OCR engines.

## Preferred capture

- Photograph one page at a time; one table per image is even better.
- Keep the camera sensor parallel to the page.
- Fill the frame while retaining all table borders.
- Use the main rear camera at original resolution.
- Avoid messaging-app compression.
- Use even diffuse light and avoid shadows.
- Keep fingers outside the table border.
- Capture a second image for important or blurred pages.
- Avoid aggressive document filters that erase decimal points or thin digits.

## Two-page spreads

Two-page spreads are acceptable when necessary, but require:

- high enough resolution for the smallest printed digit;
- visible outer borders;
- limited page curvature near the spine;
- no glare over the center gutter.

## Re-shoot criteria

Request a new image when:

- decimal points cannot be distinguished;
- rows merge after rectification;
- the page is motion-blurred;
- the table border is cut off;
- glare covers data cells;
- a cell has fewer than roughly 15—20 useful pixels in character height after cropping.
